OUR STORY

A Zambian Company With a Broad Range of Capabilities

Zorai Procurement Services Limited (ZPS) is a wholly owned Zambian company.

The company was established on 9 October 2013 and registered with the Registrar of Companies under number 116282.

ZPS sources products and services locally and internationally according to client requirements.

The company manages logistics and supply chains to deliver products and services to local, regional and international customers.

The company has grown and diversified into a broad range of products, business opportunities and relationships.
