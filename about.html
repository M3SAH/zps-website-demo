OUR STORY

A Zambian Company With a Broad Range of Capabilities

Zorai Procurement Services Limited (ZPS) is a wholly owned Zambian company established on 9 October 2013.

The company covers a broad spectrum of business activities, including the general supply of commodities and products, and sources products and services locally and internationally according to client requirements.

ZPS also manages logistics and supply chains to deliver products and services to local, regional and international customers.
