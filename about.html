Company Story

Established to deliver dependable sourcing and supply support

Zorai Procurement Services Limited is a wholly owned Zambian company established on 9 October 2013 and registered with the Registrar of Companies.

The company covers a broad spectrum of business activities, including general supply of commodities, military apparel, white goods, diesel and petrol, tyres, food rations and commodities for import and export.

ZPS sources products and services locally and internationally according to client requirements and manages logistics and supply chains to support effective delivery.

Our Mission

Effective sourcing for competitive value

ZPS aims to source and supply high-quality products and services cost-effectively to local, regional and international businesses, nongovernmental organisations and governments. The company focuses on direct and efficient delivery while maintaining pricing that remains competitive.

Our Vision

Professional, efficient and customer-focused service

ZPS aims to provide professional and efficient service delivery through its broad range of business activities and connections, with a focus on effectiveness, customer satisfaction and differentiated products or services.
