WHO WE ARE

A ZAMBIAN COMPANY BUILT AROUND VALUE, RELIABILITY AND SERVICE.

Zorai Procurement Services Limited (ZPS) is a wholly owned Zambian company established on 9 October 2013 and registered with the Registrar of Companies under number 116282.

ZPS sources products and services locally and internationally according to client requirements and manages logistics and supply chains to deliver to local, regional and international customers.

PUBLIC SERVICE INFORMATION

STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP

In May 2019, Zorai Procurement Services was granted authorization by the Public Service Management Division to market its products to public service workers throughout the country.

This arrangement enabled product access through the DDAC payroll deduction facility for eligible public service workers, subject to the applicable programme arrangements.
