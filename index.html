WHO WE ARE

A Zambian Company Built Around Value, Reliability and Service

Zorai Procurement Services Limited (ZPS) is a wholly owned Zambian company established on 9 October 2013. The company operates across a broad range of business activities, sourcing products and services locally and internationally according to client requirements.

ZPS manages logistics and supply chains to deliver products and services to local, regional and international customers with efficiency and accountability.
